## Slide 8

![Week 10 slide 8](img/week10_s08.png)

Text layer:

```
3. Wing Placement
In order to have longitudinal static stability, the aircraft aerodynamic center
(neutral point) must be aft of the aircraft center of gravity
v You could place the wing based on the most aft CG location
v Alternatively, you could define a maximum allowable most aft CG location, for
which you could then place the wing
```


## Slide 9

![Week 10 slide 9](img/week10_s09.png)

Text layer:

```
4. Iterate the horizontal stability design or modify
weight distribution
v Horizontal stabilizer initially designed using guess value of l and h.
v Redesigning using actual values, also ensuring the aircraft is stable over the
entire range of h (i.e., the required CLh can be produced over the entire range of
h). If not then you need to define the maximum allow range of h.
v May also consider rearranging the weight distribution such that the actual l is
closer to the initial value of l (which is based on the optimal value)
```
